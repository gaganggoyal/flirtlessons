How to flirt over text: six rules of thumb

  • Call back real moments. A shared joke beats a generic “hey”.
  • Match their rhythm. Roughly mirror their message length and pace. No timers, no games.
  • Make plans specific. Day, time, place. Easy to say yes to.
  • Don’t chase. No reply? Give it space, try once more later, or let it go.
  • Meet sooner rather than later. Texting sets up dates; it doesn’t replace them.
  • Keep heavy topics for in person. Tone gets lost in text.

Dry texting: how to keep a conversation alive

Dry texting means replies that give the other person nothing to work with: “nice”, “lol”, “haha yeah”. It’s rarely rude on purpose, but it makes the conversation feel like a chore. The fix is three small moves: react, add, hand it back.

They: “Just got back from the gym.”

Instead of “nice”: “Respect. Leg day, or the kind where you can still walk tomorrow?”

They: “Watching a terrible film.”

Instead of “lol”: “Terrible-boring or terrible-amazing? I need the title, for research.”

They: “Work was so long today.”

Instead of “same”: “Ugh. Did it at least end with good food, or is it cereal-for-dinner level?”

If they’re the dry texter, try one or two playful questions. If the replies stay one-word, they may be busy, shy over text or not that interested. Suggest meeting or a call, or let it go; you can’t text someone into interest.

Double texting: when it’s fine

Double texting means sending another message before they’ve replied to your last one. It’s fine when:

  • A reasonable gap has passed. A day or two, not an hour.
  • You have something fresh to say. A new thought or a callback, never “??” or “hello?”.
  • It’s once. If two messages in a row go unanswered, stop. Silence is an answer too.

How to ask someone out over text

Quick answers

How soon should I text after getting their number?

The same day or the next is fine. Say where you met so they know who it is, and lead with something you talked about: “Hi, it’s Alex from the bookshop. Did you start the book you were deciding on?”

Should I use emojis when flirting over text?

A few are great for showing tone, especially playfulness. Match the other person’s style: if they barely use emojis, a wall of them can feel like a lot.
