Second dates & beyond

How to suggest a game without it being awkward

  • Keep it casual. “Okay, quick game” works better than announcing a structured activity.
  • Pick the moment. A lull in conversation or a wait for food is perfect.
  • Go first. Start with your own answer or photo so it feels easy to join in. A well-told story makes a great opener.
  • Read the room. If they’re not into it, drop it with a smile and carry on talking.

Which game for which date?

  • First date: low-key and conversational, like two truths and a lie or this or that.
  • Walks and city dates: photo missions keep you moving and laughing.
  • Dinner: order for each other, if you’re both adventurous eaters.
  • Later dates: a running scoreboard gives you built-in reasons to meet again, and something fun to text about in between.
Key ideaGames create shared moments, and shared moments become the inside jokes you text about later.

Quick answers

Are games okay on a first date?

Yes, as long as they’re light and conversational. Simple question games help break the ice and give you both something easy to talk about.

What if my date doesn’t want to play?

No problem at all. Let it go with a smile and keep the conversation going. Games are a tool for fun, not a test.
