The four beats

  1. MomentSet the scene in one sentence: where you were and what you were doing.
  2. StakesWhat you wanted, or what could go wrong.
  3. TurnThe thing that happened that nobody expected.
  4. MeaningOne line on what it taught you, or why it still makes you laugh.

A worked example

MomentLast summer a friend dared me to sign up for a six-week pottery class.

StakesI decided I’d make my mum a proper vase for her birthday, and I told her it was coming.

TurnSix weeks later I had produced exactly one object, which the teacher politely described as “an ambitious ashtray”.

MeaningShe keeps her keys in it every day, so I’m calling it a success. What’s the worst thing you’ve ever made with your hands?

It’s under a minute long, it’s honest, it shows a sense of humor and something you care about, and it ends by handing the conversation back.

Tips for telling it well

  • Keep it under two minutes. Leave them wanting the next chapter, not checking the time.
  • Exaggerate the jokes, never the facts. Honest stories hold up when someone gets to know you.
  • Pass the mic. End with a question so they can share a story of their own.
  • Rehearse with friends. Delivery gets smoother every time you tell it.

Story ideas if you think you don’t have any

  • A time you got completely lost, in a city or in a conversation.
  • Your first job and the strangest thing that happened there.
  • A trip where the plan fell apart and something better happened instead.
  • A skill you tried to learn, and how badly the first attempt went.
  • A small kindness from a stranger that you still remember.

Quick answers

What if my life feels too boring for good stories?

The best date stories are usually small: a failed recipe, a strange encounter, a moment of embarrassment. What makes them work is how you tell them, not how dramatic they are.

How long should a story on a date be?

Aim for one to two minutes. If they’re curious, they’ll ask for more, which is much better than losing their attention halfway through.
