Privacy policy

We keep this simple because we collect almost nothing.

  • No accounts, no cookies, no tracking. We do not use cookies, analytics services, advertising or tracking pixels.
  • Anonymous usage counts. To understand which lessons and tools are useful, pages send a simple anonymous count to our own server, for example “challenge completed, score 60–69, arrived from WhatsApp”. These counts contain no IP address, no device identifier and nothing you type or choose, and they can’t be linked to you or to each other.
  • Your answers stay on your device. The friend-zone self-check and the 3-message challenge run entirely in your browser. Your individual answers and replies are never sent to us or stored. Challenge share links contain only your score.
  • Theme preference. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser’s local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts and all other files are served from this website, not from external services.
  • Hosting logs. Like any website, our server may automatically record technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability. These logs are kept for a limited time and are not used to identify or profile visitors.

If we ever add other services or start collecting anything more, we will update this page first and, where required, ask for your consent.
