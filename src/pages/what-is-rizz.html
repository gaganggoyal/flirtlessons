Where the word comes from

“Rizz” is usually explained as a shortened form of charisma, although nobody is completely sure. It spread through live streams, TikTok and YouTube from around 2021, popularized by the streamer Kai Cenat, and by 2023 it was everywhere: Oxford University Press named it its Word of the Year for 2023.

It works as a noun (“she has rizz”), as a verb (“he tried to rizz her up”) and in phrases like “W rizz”.

Rizz slang, decoded

W rizz

A win: a smooth, funny or charming moment that clearly landed.

L rizz

A loss: an attempt that fell flat. Everyone has a few.

Unspoken rizz

Charm without words: a look, a smile, the way someone carries themselves.

Rizz someone up

To flirt with someone, or try to charm them.

Rizzler

Someone with a lot of rizz. Usually said with a wink.

No rizz

Awkward or dry. Most often said about yourself, as a joke.

What real rizz looks like

Take away the slang and rizz is what people have always called being good company. It usually comes down to five habits:

  1. Playful, not perfect. A light tease or a callback to something they said beats a polished line. See what flirting really is.
  2. Curious. Follow-up questions show you’re listening, and real listening is rare. Here’s how to start a conversation.
  3. Clear. People can’t respond to interest they can’t see. “I’d like to take you out” is bolder than any line.
  4. Relaxed about the outcome. You’re finding out whether you click, not auditioning. That ease is most of what people call unspoken rizz.
  5. Respectful. Reading signals and taking a “no” gracefully. Pushing past someone’s discomfort isn’t rizz; it’s the opposite. More on reading signals.

Pickup lines vs real rizz

Pickup line

“Are you a parking ticket? Because you’ve got ‘fine’ written all over you.”

Real rizz

“Okay, settle this for me: is the oat milk worth the extra?”

The line is about you performing. The second is about something you both share, it’s easy to answer and it starts a real conversation. A cheesy line can be fun as an obvious joke between people who already get along, but it rarely starts anything on its own.

Quick answers

What does W rizz mean?

W rizz means “winning rizz”: a moment of charm that clearly worked. The opposite is L rizz, an attempt that fell flat.

Is rizz the same as flirting?

Close. Rizz is the charm; flirting is one way you use it. You can have rizz with friends too: it simply means people enjoy your company.

Can you learn rizz?

Yes. Most of what looks like natural charm is practice: listening well, being a little playful and making your interest clear. Start small, somewhere the stakes are low.
